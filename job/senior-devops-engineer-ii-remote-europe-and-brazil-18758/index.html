Senior DevOps Engineer II (Remote - Europe and Brazil)

Remote - Europe, BrazilFull-timePosted 5 days ago

This is a senior infrastructure role at a fast-growing, fully remote software company that builds a content management platform used by brands and developers across the world. The engineer takes ownership of major infrastructure subsystems and multi-cycle projects that affect several engineering teams at once. The position suits someone who can turn vague problems into clear designs and who raises the standards of the people around them.

What you'll do

  • Own the technical health and long-term scalability of a defined infrastructure domain, from design through to stable production operation.
  • Lead multi-stage infrastructure projects, including architecture, implementation plans, and measurable delivery.
  • Write and review Terraform, CI/CD pipelines, and platform automation, and set standards other teams follow.
  • Find systemic reliability risks and technical debt early, and drive fixes before they affect customers.
  • Improve monitoring, observability, and incident response, and take part in root cause analysis.
  • Mentor mid-level and senior engineers and act as a technical reference point for the infrastructure area.

What you need

  • At least 9 years of experience as a DevOps, Infrastructure, or Site Reliability Engineer, with clear ownership of complex systems.
  • Strong experience running large-scale applications on AWS; GCP or Azure experience is a plus.
  • Deep Infrastructure-as-Code skills, mainly Terraform, including reusable modules and state management.
  • Solid knowledge of container and serverless platforms such as ECS, EKS, Fargate, or Lambda, plus Linux, networking, and scripting in Bash, Python, Go, or Ruby.
  • Experience with PostgreSQL operations, observability tools (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog), SLOs, and security practices such as IAM, secrets management, and vulnerability management.
  • Excellent written and spoken English and the ability to work well in a remote-first team.

Applicants must be based in: United Kingdom, Germany, France, Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Poland, Ireland, Italy, Belgium, Brazil.
