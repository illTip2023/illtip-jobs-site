Copywriter (Port Harcourt)

Port HarcourtFull-time, On-sitePosted 10 days ago

This is a full-time, on-site copywriting role based in Port Harcourt. It suits a confident writer who enjoys shaping clear, persuasive words for brands and audiences, and who understands how people make choices online. The organisation is a business that relies on written content to reach its customers, so the work values creativity backed by careful research. Candidates with a solid academic background, proven experience writing marketing copy, and a habit of meeting deadlines will find this a good fit for a steady, hands-on writing position.

What you'll do

  • Write persuasive marketing copy for digital and print channels
  • Research audiences, products and market trends to inform content
  • Apply consumer psychology to craft messages that connect with readers
  • Work to deadlines across multiple writing projects
  • Collaborate on campaign ideas and creative direction

What you need

  • Graduate qualification
  • Proven copywriting experience
  • Excellent writing and communication skills
  • Understanding of digital marketing and consumer psychology
  • Creativity and strong research skills
  • Ability to meet deadlines
