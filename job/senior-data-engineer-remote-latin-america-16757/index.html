Senior Data Engineer (Remote - Latin America)

Remote - Latin AmericaFull-time contract (one-year project, with possible extension)$36,000–$54,000 per yearPosted 4 days ago

This is a remote contract role placed through a talent services firm that connects Latin American developers and designers with companies abroad. The successful candidate will be placed with a client company and work day to day on its data platform. The role combines hands-on engineering with data modeling and reporting, and it suits someone who enjoys both building pipelines and communicating with business and IT stakeholders.

What you'll do

  • Design, build and maintain data pipelines and analytics solutions on Databricks
  • Write and tune SQL (including Spark SQL) and Python for data processing and analysis
  • Work alongside developers and IT teams to deliver data analytics projects
  • Apply DevOps practices and CI/CD methods to data workflows
  • Build and support Power BI reports and dashboards
  • Contribute to data modeling, data management and data governance standards

What you need

  • At least 5 years of experience as a data analyst, data engineer or similar role
  • Background in software development and data-related IT projects
  • Practical programming skills in SQL and Python, and experience working with developers
  • Extensive hands-on experience with Databricks and advanced user-level experience with cloud platforms
  • Working knowledge of DevOps tools and CI/CD, plus experience with Power BI and solid understanding of data modeling and governance
  • Fluent, business-level English and residence in Latin America

Applicants must be based in: Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Peru.
