Internal Customer Success Manager (Remote - Worldwide)

Remote - WorldwideFull-timePosted 9 days ago

Work must align with US business hours, with responsiveness outside core hours when clients need it. This is a full-time remote role with a fast-growing offshore staffing company that places full-time overseas talent with founders and executives. The position looks after existing client accounts after the sale, which is where most of the company's new business comes from through referrals and repeat work. The team is small, with direct access to the co-founders and little separation between this role and key business decisions.

What you'll do

  • Act as the main contact for existing clients once a placement or engagement begins
  • Join kickoff, check-in and escalation calls and manage each client relationship through the engagement
  • Run regular check-ins across active accounts and catch problems with placed contractors before clients raise them
  • Assess the top candidates the recruiting team surfaces for each role, choose who is ready to present, and manage interviews, feedback and closing
  • Identify clients who are ready to hire again, and ask satisfied clients for reviews and introductions
  • Send personalized gestures for milestones and first placements to build long-term relationships

What you need

  • Relevant experience in customer success, account management, staffing or recruiting
  • Strong critical thinking and the ability to make and defend a clear recommendation on candidates and client issues
  • Full ownership mindset, treating client outcomes as your responsibility until they are resolved
  • Clear, personal written communication and comfort with calls
  • Ability to work US business hours and respond promptly outside them when clients need it
  • Comfortable working in a role that is still taking shape and expanding over time

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
