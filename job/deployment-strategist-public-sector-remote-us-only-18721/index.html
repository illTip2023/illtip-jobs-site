Deployment Strategist, Public Sector (Remote - US only)

Remote - US onlyFull-timePosted 5 days ago

Preference for candidates based in the Washington, D.C. area. Regular travel for customer onsites, including secure facilities for federal work. You will join an AI audio research and product company that sells an enterprise AI agent platform for voice and chat. Your focus is the US federal government and state, local and education (SLED) customers. You will work alongside forward deployed engineers, go-to-market staff, product engineers and other strategists to turn agency and school system needs into a deployed, adopted solution. The role is a mix of customer discovery, deal shaping, technical demos and delivery ownership.

What you'll do

  • Find high-impact uses for AI agents in citizen-facing, student-facing and internal operational workflows such as benefits, licensing, 311 and contact centers, case management, admissions and service desks.
  • Own priority deals from use case through close, including commercial structure and navigating public procurement routes such as federal contract vehicles, cooperative contracts and RFPs.
  • Embed with customer teams, including onsite time in secure facilities, and take ownership of delivery outcomes.
  • Advise customers on agent design, orchestration and production readiness, including the security, privacy and compliance rules that apply to each segment.
  • Work with engineering teams to build and demo solutions, present to audiences from technical staff to agency heads and elected or appointed officials, and feed field insights back to product and research.

What you need

  • U.S. citizenship and an active U.S. government security clearance for federal engagements.
  • At least 3 years in a customer-facing technical role with government agencies, a public sector systems integrator or a government-focused technology company.
  • Working knowledge of public sector buying and compliance, such as GSA schedules, OTAs, FedRAMP and ATO processes, state cooperative contracts, and FERPA, CJIS or HIPAA where relevant.
  • Comfort with long enterprise sales cycles and deal conversations with partners.
  • Basic Python and API integration skills, enough to prototype and talk credibly with engineers, plus familiarity with LLM and AI agent frameworks.
  • Strong communication and problem-solving skills, with a record of carrying ambiguous, complex projects to delivery.

Applicants must be based in: United States.
