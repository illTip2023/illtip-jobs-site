Technical Writer (Visa Sponsorship - San Francisco, USA)

San Francisco, United StatesFull-time$100,000–$165,000 per yearPosted 7 days ago

Visa sponsorship available. A technical content team at an AI company that builds tools for reinforcement learning and model evaluation is hiring a Technical Writer. You will turn product progress, customer workflows, and research into clear, evidence-based content for AI engineers, researchers, and technical leaders. The role sits close to founders and cross-functional teammates, so you will help set a consistent voice for the company's technical communication.

What you'll do

  • Write case studies, technical articles, product explainers, launch materials, and short posts for X and LinkedIn.
  • Interview customers, partners, and internal experts, and turn demos and technical conversations into stories backed by concrete results.
  • Learn the platform, marketplace, and customer workflows well enough to explain them accurately.
  • Work with product, marketing, and leadership to communicate product vision and technical outcomes consistently.
  • Propose content topics and formats, and manage drafts from first idea through review and publication.
  • Refine work using reader engagement data and team feedback.

What you need

  • Experience writing technical content for engineering or AI audiences, supported by a portfolio that explains complex topics clearly.
  • Strong interviewing and storytelling skills, with the ability to draw out meaningful technical detail.
  • Ability to pick up new technologies and platforms quickly and make them accessible.
  • Experience writing for social channels and managing projects from idea to publication.
  • Comfort collaborating with product, marketing, and customer-facing teams.
  • Must be able to work on-site in San Francisco.
