Fullstack Software Engineer - Growth (Remote, France, Belgium or Spain)

Remote · Belgium, SpainFull-timePosted 4 days ago

You must be legally eligible to work in one of these three countries. A fast-growing health insurance and prevention company is hiring a senior fullstack engineer for its Growth engineering team. The work sits at the crossroads of product and marketing: you will build the flows that turn website visitors into members and the systems that drive acquisition across paid, SEO and other channels. The company operates in France, Spain, Belgium and Canada, and the team ships to production daily.

What you'll do

  • Build and improve self-serve sign-up flows, from discovery and quote through to final enrolment, with a focus on conversion.
  • Develop conversational AI features, including voice and chat assistants that guide prospective customers through their questions.
  • Build and maintain the company website, which handles high traffic across all markets.
  • Create and scale marketing systems and tracking that support paid and organic acquisition in several countries.
  • Work closely with marketing, sales, research, product, data and design colleagues, and take ownership of what you ship.

What you need

  • 6+ years of professional experience building and shipping web applications.
  • Strong experience with React and Python (Flask is a plus) and relational databases such as PostgreSQL.
  • Interest in conversion funnels, marketing metrics and how product changes affect growth.
  • Ability to work independently, take ownership and collaborate well in a team that also teaches and learns from each other.
  • Working proficiency in English.
  • Legal right to work in France, Belgium or Spain.

Applicants must be based in: France, Spain, Belgium.
