Lead Research Engineer, Data Quality (Visa Sponsorship - United States)

United StatesFull-time$130,000–$225,000 per yearPosted 8 days ago

An early-stage AI company building infrastructure for training and evaluating AI systems is hiring a leader for its data quality team. This person will shape how training data is measured and improved, and help build reliable systems for evaluating agent tasks and producing high-value post-training data. The role is on-site in the United States in a small, fast-moving startup environment.

What you'll do

  • Lead systems that evaluate tasks across reinforcement learning environments, synthetic data, benchmarks, and domain-specific workflows.
  • Define data quality standards and build quality-control systems, including experiments that assess agent outputs.
  • Develop scalable methods to validate synthetic data, such as failure-mode analysis, task mutation checks, and trajectory audits.
  • Work with research engineers, subject-matter experts, and data vendors to find quality issues and improve data generation workflows.
  • Turn research findings into production tools, dashboards, validation pipelines, and feedback loops.
  • Mentor research engineers and encourage rigorous, clear, and efficient technical work.

What you need

  • At least 5 years of research or engineering experience, including building AI or machine learning data evaluation systems.
  • Prior experience leading technical projects or teams from problem definition through implementation and iteration.
  • Advanced proficiency in Python, Docker, and Linux, with a technical academic or professional background.
  • Strong understanding of AI evaluation and post-training, and of what makes agent training data realistic, learnable, diverse, reliable, and useful.
  • Experience with synthetic data validation, evaluation infrastructure, benchmarks, metrics, and quality-control workflows.
  • Clear written communication, strong attention to detail, and the ability to work independently.
