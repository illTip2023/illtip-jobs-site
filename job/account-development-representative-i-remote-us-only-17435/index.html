Account Development Representative I (Remote - US only)

Remote - US onlyFull-time$91,000–$181,700 per yearPosted 8 days ago

This is an entry-level outbound and inbound sales development role at a fast-growing enterprise software company focused on process orchestration. The team is collaborative and metrics-driven, and it shares wins openly. You will book qualified meetings and build pipeline for Account Executives, with a clear path to learn the fundamentals of tech sales in an enterprise setting.

What you'll do

  • Run outbound prospecting across email, phone, LinkedIn, and event outreach into assigned target accounts.
  • Qualify inbound interest through scheduled calls and convert qualified leads into meetings for Account Executives.
  • Research accounts, buyer personas, and business signals to tailor outreach, using AI tools to speed up research.
  • Work with Account Executives to define what a quality meeting, contact, and account looks like, and adjust your approach based on their feedback.
  • Keep accurate activity and pipeline records in the CRM and sales tools to track your own performance.
  • Learn the product and its value propositions well enough to hold useful conversations with prospects in specific industries.

What you need

  • Based in the United States; the role is open only to US-based candidates.
  • Some experience in outbound prospecting or customer-facing work is preferred; this can come from roles in retail, hospitality, customer support, or internships.
  • Strong time management and the discipline to complete high volumes of calls and emails.
  • Curiosity about technology and willingness to learn a technical product; no engineering background required.
  • Resilience, coachability, and good cross-team communication.
  • Ability to maintain a dedicated remote setup for consistent weekly work and to travel about 4-6 times per year, usually for a week or less at a time.

Applicants must be based in: United States.
