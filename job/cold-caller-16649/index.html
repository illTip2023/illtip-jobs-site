Cold Caller

Remote WorldwidePart-timePosted 5 days ago

A remote part-time role supporting a recruitment and staffing business that places talent with client companies. The work centres on phoning organisations that are hiring or may need staff, introducing the agency's services and keeping in touch with prospects so the business can follow up and close deals. It suits someone who is comfortable on the phone, speaks clearly and confidently, and is organised with spreadsheets. Candidates should be comfortable working with a calling tool and building steady relationships with businesses.

What you'll do

  • Call companies that are hiring or may be looking to hire
  • Introduce the staffing agency's services to prospective clients
  • Follow up and nurture leads over time
  • Keep accurate records of calls and outcomes in spreadsheets
  • Pass warm prospects on for the recruiter to close

What you need

  • Confident, clear speaking voice
  • Familiarity with spreadsheets
  • Comfortable using a dialer system such as Google Voice
  • Self-motivated and able to work remotely
  • Part-time availability

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
