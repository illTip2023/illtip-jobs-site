Research Engineer, Data & Evals (Remote - Worldwide)

Remote - WorldwideFull-time$230,000–$450,000 per yearPosted 5 days ago

This is a research engineering role at a fast-growing AI company building agentic systems for legal and professional services work. The team recently post-trained its first model for legal tasks and is now expanding its post-training effort. The role focuses on the data and measurement infrastructure behind training environments, benchmarks, and evaluations, where correctness is hard to verify automatically.

What you'll do

  • Build synthetic data pipelines that generate, filter, and curate training and evaluation samples for complex legal reasoning and agentic workflows.
  • Design evaluations and verifiers that reflect real legal work, calibrate them against expert judgment, and investigate where they fail.
  • Build and maintain capability benchmarks and product-focused evals that track model performance on real customer workflows.
  • Work with legal domain experts to define quality metrics, rubrics, and grading mechanisms.
  • Own training and evaluation data quality, including provenance, coverage, and safeguards against contamination.
  • Partner with researchers to investigate model failures and separate real gains from misleading metrics.

What you need

  • Experience building data pipelines, ML evaluation systems, or experimentation tooling, with strong Python skills and production-quality code.
  • A rigorous approach to measurement, including statistical reasoning, experimental design, and an understanding of when metrics mislead.
  • Ability to turn ambiguous goals or expert judgment into structured datasets, rubrics, and measurable criteria.
  • Experience with synthetic data, LLM evaluations, automated raters, or preference data is a plus; strong data and measurement engineering close to ML development is valued even without prior LLM work.
  • Comfort working closely with researchers and legal experts in a small, high-ownership team.

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
