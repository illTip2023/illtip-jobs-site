Senior Recruiter

Remote WorldwidePosted Yesterday

This is a senior recruitment role within a recruitment process outsourcing team, supporting a client organisation in New Jersey on a hybrid schedule. The work suits a strategic, relationship-driven recruiter who enjoys building trusted partnerships with hiring managers, designing sourcing strategies and mapping markets to find great talent. The organisation is a global talent and leadership advisory firm working with clients across many sectors. The role is well suited to an experienced full-cycle recruiter with a commercial or corporate hiring background who wants to create lasting talent pipelines and make a visible impact.

What you'll do

  • Manage the full recruitment cycle for roles supporting a client team
  • Build sourcing strategies and map talent markets
  • Partner closely with clients and hiring managers
  • Use Boolean search and other sourcing methods to identify candidates
  • Build community partnerships and sustainable talent pipelines
  • Maintain accurate candidate records in the applicant tracking system

What you need

  • 5+ years of full-cycle recruiting and sourcing experience
  • Corporate or commercial recruiting experience preferred
  • Strong client and hiring manager partnership skills
  • Expertise in sourcing strategy, market mapping and Boolean search
  • Applicant tracking system experience, preferably Workday
  • Experience building community partnerships and talent pipelines

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
