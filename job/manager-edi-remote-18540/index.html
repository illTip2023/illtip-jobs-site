Manager, EDI (Remote)

RemoteFull-time$110,625–$147,500 per yearPosted 8 days ago

A fast-growing health benefits company focused on serving small and midsize businesses and their employees is hiring a leader to run its electronic data interchange (EDI) function. The role owns how eligibility, enrollment and claims data move between the company, its trading partners and internal systems as its book of business expands. The manager will lead a team that configures, tests and monitors these connections and will set the standards for accuracy, security and scale. The position reports to the Director of Configuration.

What you'll do

  • Lead, hire, coach and develop a team of EDI analysts, and cross-train so no critical process depends on a single person.
  • Own the EDI program standards (file specifications, mapping, naming, scheduling and documentation) and keep an up-to-date inventory of every trading partner, file and schedule with a named owner.
  • Manage new trading partner setup from requirements through testing, UAT and go-live, turning business needs from product, enrollment, operations and finance into technical specifications.
  • Oversee daily monitoring of inbound and outbound X12 transactions (834, 837, 835, 278 and acknowledgments such as 999, TA1 and 277CA) and drive rejects and failures to root cause.
  • Manage trading partner, clearinghouse and vendor relationships, including SLA performance and escalations, and report on volumes, error rates, turnaround times and risk to leadership.

What you need

  • 7+ years of healthcare EDI experience, including 2+ years leading a team in EDI, configuration, claims operations or healthcare IT.
  • Deep working knowledge of HIPAA X12 standards (834, 837, 835, 278) and acknowledgments, and how they connect to enrollment and claims adjudication.
  • Hands-on experience validating, mapping and troubleshooting files, including reading raw X12 and identifying loop and segment errors.
  • Experience in benefits administration, eligibility and enrollment, or claims processing, ideally within a TPA, payer or carrier.
  • Working knowledge of SFTP and secure file transfer practices, and a commitment to HIPAA and privacy requirements.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to explain technical issues to non-technical stakeholders.
