Junior Accountant (Remote - US only)

Remote - US onlyFull-time$45,000–$55,000 per yearPosted 8 days ago

A established health IT company that provides clinical communication and physician scheduling software to medical practices, hospitals and health systems is hiring a Junior Accountant to support day-to-day accounting operations. The team is remote-first and places a strong emphasis on accuracy, growth and collaboration across Accounting and Finance. Pay within the range depends on experience, skills and the team the hire supports.

What you'll do

  • Process and apply customer payments received by ACH, wire, lockbox and credit card across multiple companies and bank accounts
  • Research and resolve unapplied cash, unidentified receipts, payment discrepancies and posting errors
  • Prepare and enter journal entries in NetSuite with proper supporting documentation
  • Produce monthly and ad hoc financial reports and review outstanding customer credits on accounts receivable aging reports
  • Support the escheatment process, including researching outstanding balances and maintaining compliance records
  • Perform assigned balance sheet reconciliations and assist with month-end close, working with the Collections team on customer payment issues

What you need

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field
  • 1-2 years of accounting experience, ideally in a corporate setting
  • Experience with accounts receivable, cash application, journal entries or account reconciliations is a plus; NetSuite experience is preferred but not required
  • Strong Excel skills and proficiency in Microsoft Office
  • Solid grasp of basic accounting principles, with excellent attention to detail and the ability to manage deadlines
  • Good communication skills and the ability to work both independently and within a team

Applicants must be based in: United States.
