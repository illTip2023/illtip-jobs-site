Graphic Designer, Brand & Marketing (Remote - Philippines and South Africa)

Remote - Philippines and South AfricaPart-time$6,000–$9,600 per yearPosted 11 days ago

This is a part-time remote role for a graphic designer who will create brand-aligned visuals, marketing presentations and print-ready production artwork for a company that works with external suppliers. The position combines conceptual design with technical file preparation. You will work from briefs and existing brand guidelines to deliver polished work from first concept through to final delivery.

What you'll do

  • Design brand and marketing visuals, including customer-facing sales and marketing presentations
  • Produce and refine AI-generated imagery and other creative assets to brand standards
  • Prepare design files and artwork so they are technically correct and ready for suppliers and production
  • Apply templates and brand guidelines consistently across all materials
  • Manage several creative projects and deadlines at once, communicating with internal teams and external suppliers

What you need

  • Previous professional experience in graphic design
  • Strong grasp of design principles, layout and visual composition
  • Experience building marketing presentations or sales decks
  • Working knowledge of AI-generated imagery and AI creative tools
  • Familiarity with Adobe Creative Cloud (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign) or Canva; experience with packaging, print or label artwork is a strong advantage
  • Strong written and spoken English, with excellent organisation and attention to detail
  • Must be based in the Philippines or South Africa to apply

Applicants must be based in: South Africa, Philippines.
