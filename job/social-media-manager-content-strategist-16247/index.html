Social Media Manager / Content Strategist

Abuja, NigeriaPosted 11 days ago

An onsite role in Abuja for a creative professional who can plan and produce engaging content across major social platforms. The organisation works in the property sector, so the role blends storytelling with audience growth and lead generation. It suits a confident writer with several years of social media experience, a strong eye for design and video, and an interest in real estate and property investment.

What you'll do

  • Develop creative, engaging content tailored to different social media platforms
  • Plan and manage content schedules using scheduling tools
  • Track social media analytics and use insights to improve performance
  • Grow audience engagement and support lead generation
  • Produce copy, stories and short-form video content

What you need

  • Strong writing, copywriting and storytelling skills
  • Working knowledge of Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube
  • At least three years' experience in social media management and content creation
  • A portfolio of previous content and social media work
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail
  • Basic graphic design and short-form video editing skills are an advantage
