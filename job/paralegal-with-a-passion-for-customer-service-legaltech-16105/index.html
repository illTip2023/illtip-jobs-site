Paralegal with a Passion for Customer Service (LegalTech)

Remote WorldwideFull-timePosted 12 days ago

This remote, full-time role sits at the meeting point of legal work and customer care within a technology-focused legal services business. It suits a mid-level paralegal who enjoys working with clients as much as with documents, and who is comfortable with software tools used in modern legal practice. The organisation works remotely across borders, so the role is open to candidates in many countries. It is a good fit for someone with strong written English, careful attention to detail, and an interest in supporting customers through the practical side of contracts and reporting.

What you'll do

  • Draft customer agreements and reports
  • Provide customer-facing support
  • Offer some support to the sales function
  • Work with legal technology tools in day-to-day tasks

What you need

  • Mid-level paralegal experience
  • Familiarity with LegalTech tools
  • Strong legal drafting skills
  • Excellent written English
  • Detail-oriented approach
  • Comfort with customer-facing work

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
