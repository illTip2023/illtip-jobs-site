Performance Assurance Analyst, Federal Health IT (Remote - US only)

Remote - US onlyNot specified in postingPosted 13 days ago

A technology services company that builds and supports mission-critical software for federal organizations is hiring a Performance Assurance Analyst for its federal healthcare technology work. The role independently measures and verifies service performance so that customers receive accurate, evidence-based results. The analyst stays separate from the engineering team that builds the data pipelines, providing an objective check on reported outcomes.

What you'll do

  • Calculate experience-level, service-level and KPI results from customer-owned data using established measurement definitions.
  • Prepare monthly and quarterly performance measurements with traceable calculations and supporting documentation.
  • Maintain a performance exception register covering service delays, measurement pauses, missing data and baseline-related exceptions.
  • Audit data pipeline outputs against source records to confirm accuracy, completeness and consistency.
  • Document and report discrepancies, and work with responsible teams to resolve them.
  • Build reconciliation packages for authorized customer review, and support quarterly performance reviews with clear findings.

What you need

  • At least 3 years of professional experience in performance measurement, audit or quality assurance on a service contract.
  • Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Power BI for analyzing, validating and presenting performance data.
  • Eligibility for a Tier 3 federal investigation (personnel vetting).
  • Must be located in the United States.

Applicants must be based in: United States.
