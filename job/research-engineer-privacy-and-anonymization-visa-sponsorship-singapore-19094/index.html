Research Engineer, Privacy and Anonymization (Visa Sponsorship - Singapore)

Singapore, SingaporeFull-time$130,000–$225,000 per yearPosted Yesterday

Visa sponsorship available. A company building data tooling for AI teams is hiring a research engineer to design and run systems that remove or disguise sensitive information in real-world datasets before those datasets are used for model training, evaluation, or synthetic data generation. The work balances protecting people's private information with keeping data useful for the downstream work that depends on it. This is an on-site position based in Singapore.

What you'll do

  • Build systems that detect personal identifiers, quasi-identifiers, credentials, and other sensitive content, and choose the right transformation for each data type and use case.
  • Develop and benchmark detection approaches that combine rules, statistical models, classifiers, and language models.
  • Create production pipelines that anonymize data before it reaches processing, training, or evaluation stages.
  • Build evaluation frameworks for privacy risk and data utility, including recall-focused metrics, leakage tests, and adversarial re-identification attempts.
  • Design pipelines that cope with new data sources, schema changes, unusual formats, and sensitive values in unexpected fields.
  • Work with engineering, research, operations, and customer teams to turn privacy requirements into practical safeguards.

What you need

  • A technical degree and at least 2 years of experience building production data or machine learning systems in Python.
  • Hands-on experience detecting, removing, or anonymizing PII while preserving useful data characteristics.
  • Experience with information extraction, named-entity recognition, or classification for rare or sensitive content.
  • Experience building end-to-end data pipelines and comparing approaches on recall, precision, latency, cost, and downstream utility.
  • Working knowledge of redaction, masking, pseudonymization, anonymization, and synthetic data.
  • Valuable: experience with sensitive data in healthcare, finance, or security; knowledge of privacy-enhancing technologies such as differential privacy, k-anonymity, secure aggregation, or format-preserving encryption; experience with low-latency or high-throughput ML inference.
