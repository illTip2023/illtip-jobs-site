Technical Talent Acquisition Partner (Mid-Level, Remote - Worldwide) (not specified in posting) · Remote - Worldwide. The full job description, the employer and how to apply are in the i'llTip app.

What you'll do

  • Own the full recruitment cycle for technical positions, from sourcing and screening through interviews, offer negotiation and placement.
  • Source candidates using traditional and newer methods, including reaching out to passive talent.
  • Use AI tools and automation for candidate sourcing, talent research, profile analysis and workflow improvements.
  • Partner with hiring managers and clients to clarify role requirements, share progress updates and keep hiring on track.
  • Build and maintain working knowledge of technical areas such as software development, data, cloud, DevOps and cybersecurity.
  • Track KPIs, keep accurate records in the applicant tracking system, and suggest ways to improve sourcing and hiring speed.

What you need

  • 2 to 5 years of technical recruitment or talent acquisition experience, ideally in a tech company, recruitment agency, outsourcing or RPO setting.
  • Proven record of managing end-to-end hiring for technical roles.
  • Hands-on experience with sourcing, Boolean search, LinkedIn Recruiter and candidate engagement.
  • Experience using AI-powered recruiting tools or automation, and applicant tracking systems.
  • Strong written and spoken communication, organisation and relationship-building skills.

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
