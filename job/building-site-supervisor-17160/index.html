Building Site Supervisor

Lagos Island, Lagos State NigeriaPosted 4 days ago

A building construction role based on Lagos Island, Lagos State, overseeing day-to-day work on construction projects. The organisation runs building projects that require careful coordination of workers, contractors and materials to deliver quality results on schedule. This role suits an experienced construction professional who reads drawings confidently, leads teams well and takes pride in sound workmanship and site safety.

What you'll do

  • Oversee daily construction activities so work follows approved building plans and specifications
  • Coordinate workers, contractors and materials to keep the project running smoothly
  • Interpret building and structural drawings and check that construction matches the approved designs
  • Monitor construction materials, take inventory, track usage and account for site resources
  • Supervise site workers, track progress and enforce safety standards
  • Help ensure the project meets its timelines and quality standards

What you need

  • Proven experience in building construction and site or project management
  • Ability to read, interpret and understand architectural, structural and construction drawings
  • Good knowledge of building structures, construction processes and quality control
  • Strong leadership, communication and workforce management skills
  • Ability to coordinate site workers and contractors
  • Must be based on Lagos Island
