Product Management Intern (Visa Sponsorship - Espoo, Finland)

Espoo, FinlandFull-time, fixed-term internship (3-6 months, flexible length)Posted 11 days ago

Visa sponsorship available. A fast-growing Finnish scale-up in the space-based data industry is offering a fully paid onsite product management internship for students who want to start a product career working on real problems. No prior product management experience is required. Interns join a product team as contributing members on a live project, with a dedicated manager and a mentor. The internship starts in April or early May 2027.

What you'll do

  • Own a defined part of a live product project and help decide what the team builds next and why
  • Talk with customers, users and internal experts, and turn their problems into clear requirements
  • Work with engineers and designers from problem definition through to delivery and measuring results
  • Use data and AI tools to analyse problems, set priorities and explain the tradeoffs behind recommendations
  • Work with sales, solutions and operations teams to get finished work into customers' hands
  • Present findings and plans in weekly reviews and demo days

What you need

  • Currently enrolled as a Bachelor's student (3rd year or later) or a Master's student, ideally in engineering, computer science, data science or physics; recent graduates are also eligible
  • Strong academic record and a track record of standing out through projects, internships or student communities
  • Ability to break down problems, design an analysis and test assumptions with data
  • Comfortable using AI tools such as AI assistants and coding agents to speed up research and analysis
  • Fluent written and spoken English and strong communication skills
  • Eligible to work onsite in Finland (EU/EEA citizen, or able to obtain a work visa with employer support). Applicants may be subject to security screening.
