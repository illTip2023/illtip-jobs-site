Senior Product Manager, Customer Experience (Remote, Canada)

Remote, CanadaFull-time$120,120–$223,080 per yearPosted Yesterday

A Canadian SaaS company that helps small service businesses run their operations is hiring a Senior Product Manager for its Customer Experience team. CX owns in-product support, sign-up and onboarding, checkout, customer profiles, success playbooks, and AI-powered tools that help customers grow. The team works horizontally across product, engineering, design, and customer-facing departments. The role reports to the VP, Customer Experience Innovation.

What you'll do

  • Lead end-to-end product work on key CX initiatives, from discovery and strategy through delivery.
  • Talk regularly with service providers and with Marketing, Support, and Success teams to understand pain points firsthand.
  • Use data and customer research to pick the highest-impact opportunities, size them, and measure results.
  • Shape how AI supports customers and staff, including when to automate, when to hand off to a person, and how quality and trust are measured.
  • Define and run the roadmap, balancing short-term wins with longer-term innovation, and report progress to senior leadership.
  • Partner with other product teams and with Customer Success, Support, Marketing, Sales, and Revenue Operations to set priorities.

What you need

  • Proven experience as a Senior Product Manager at a SaaS or technology company with measurable outcomes.
  • Experience owning customer-facing products in onboarding, adoption, retention, or support.
  • Strong analytical skills and comfort working directly with data.
  • Track record of leading through influence on horizontal or platform teams.
  • Ability to communicate across technical and non-technical audiences and to work through trade-offs with engineers.
  • Must be based in Canada.

Applicants must be based in: Canada.
