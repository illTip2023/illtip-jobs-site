Administrative Assistant (Remote - US only)

Remote - US onlyFull-timePosted Today

This is a full-time remote administrative role supporting the Government Affairs and Marketing & Communications teams at a fast-growing energy technology organization. The role is posted on behalf of a partner company that handles all applications and next steps. You will keep two busy departments running smoothly, covering scheduling, events, reporting support and stakeholder coordination. The work suits someone who is discreet, highly organized and comfortable with shifting priorities.

What you'll do

  • Split administrative support roughly 60% to Government Affairs and 40% to Marketing & Communications, covering calendars, meetings, travel and briefing materials.
  • Help prepare and submit state and federal lobbying reports, and keep accurate records of lobbying activity, expenses, registrations and deadlines.
  • Plan and support meetings, receptions and briefings with stakeholders such as community leaders and elected officials, including event setup and breakdown.
  • Coordinate event registrations, sponsored-event materials, facility tour logistics, expense reports and purchase orders.
  • Draft routine correspondence, invitations, meeting confirmations and thank-you notes, and prepare talking points and contact lists.

What you need

  • High school diploma or GED required; an associate's or bachelor's degree is preferred.
  • Around 5-7 years of administrative support and/or customer service experience.
  • Strong proficiency in Microsoft Outlook, Excel, Word and PowerPoint; familiarity with SharePoint or similar document and cloud storage systems is a plus.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills and the ability to handle confidential information with discretion.
  • Strong organizational skills, attention to detail and the ability to manage competing deadlines independently.
  • Ability to travel occasionally (up to about 20%) and to work occasional overtime, weekends or adjusted schedules. Must be able to meet U.S. Department of Energy authorization requirements for access to export-controlled information (10 CFR Part 810).

Applicants must be based in: United States.
