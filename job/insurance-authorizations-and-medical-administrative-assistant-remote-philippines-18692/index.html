Insurance Authorizations & Medical Administrative Assistant (Remote, Philippines)

Remote, PhilippinesFull-time (40 hours per week)Posted 6 days ago

Work hours follow US Central Time (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM CST), which means overnight shifts in the Philippines. A US-based psychiatry and behavioral health practice serving patients in Louisiana is hiring a remote administrative professional to handle insurance benefits checks, prior authorizations, new-patient chart preparation and front-desk phone work. The role is a daily operational position that helps make sure patients have coverage confirmed and paperwork ready before their visits. The practice works in eClinicalWorks and Google Workspace.

What you'll do

  • Verify mental health and behavioral health benefits through commercial, Medicare and Medicaid payer portals, including deductibles and copays.
  • Submit, track and follow up on prior authorizations for psychiatric evaluations, treatments and medications until approved.
  • Build and audit new-patient evaluation charts in eClinicalWorks ahead of scheduled visits, including intake forms, demographics, insurance cards and referrals.
  • Calculate patient balances from verified benefits, collect copays and outstanding payments by phone, and post them accurately.
  • Handle inbound and outbound calls to schedule new evaluations and follow-ups, run recall outreach for overdue patients, and route prescription refill requests per practice protocol.

What you need

  • At least two years of medical administrative experience in a psychiatry, mental health or behavioral health setting.
  • Hands-on experience with eClinicalWorks for scheduling, chart prep, payment posting and refill workflows.
  • Proven experience verifying insurance eligibility and submitting prior authorizations through payer portals.
  • Fluent, clear, accent-neutral English, both spoken and written.
  • Strong skills in Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs and Google Sheets.
  • Full availability for the US Central Time schedule, with no other clients or employment during these hours. Must provide proof of separation from any previous employer before onboarding.
  • A private, quiet home workspace that supports HIPAA confidentiality, with a reliable computer, backup internet or power, and a noise-canceling headset.

Applicants must be based in: Philippines.
