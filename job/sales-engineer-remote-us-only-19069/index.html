Sales Engineer (Remote - US only)

Remote - US onlyFull-time$125,000–$150,000 per yearPosted 2 days ago

A fast-growing AI platform serving the compliance space, used by thousands of organizations to run proactive compliance, training, hotline and analytics programs. This is an individual contributor sales engineering role that partners with account executives and customer success on technical sales cycles. The company is fully remote and values high autonomy, with team meetings scheduled to fit North American time zones.

What you'll do

  • Own the technical side of sales opportunities from discovery and demo planning through evaluation, objection handling and close, working alongside account executives
  • Run technical discovery and deliver tailored product demos that map platform capabilities to buyer pain points for HR, IT, security and legal stakeholders
  • Build custom walkthroughs, integrations and proofs of concept for high-priority deals, and keep the demo environment current with new features
  • Answer technical questions on data security, single sign-on and HRIS integrations, and complete RFPs, security reviews and technical questionnaires
  • Create sales-ready assets such as talk tracks, video walkthroughs and objection-handling guides for the wider sales team
  • Relay field feedback to product and engineering teams

What you need

  • Hands-on experience in sales engineering, solutions engineering or pre-sales for B2B SaaS, or a closely related technical role (implementation, technical solutions, technical customer success) where you have run discovery and demos during live deals
  • Strong technical curiosity with experience working with APIs, integrations (HRIS, LMS, SSO) and AI tools; comfortable prototyping quick scripts or tools
  • Ability to explain technical concepts simply to non-technical buyers
  • Comfortable in fast-changing, startup-style environments with limited established process
  • Self-directed, able to work independently with minimal supervision
  • Must be based in the United States

Applicants must be based in: United States.
