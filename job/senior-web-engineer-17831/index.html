Senior Web Engineer

Remote LagosPosted 3 days ago

This is a senior engineering role on a web engineering team at a technology company, based in Lagos and open to remote working. The team works on complex products in fast-growing areas of technology. The role suits an experienced engineer who enjoys owning large projects, guiding technical decisions, and helping colleagues grow. Strong communication, a self-motivated approach, and a commitment to quality and collaboration are central to the position. The company values transparency, intellectual honesty and teamwork.

What you'll do

  • Own the development of large projects and drive team initiatives to completion
  • Design and implement well-tested, well-documented software
  • Lead engineering processes through code and architectural reviews
  • Debug and fix issues reported by users
  • Contribute to technical documentation and improve engineering and operations practices
  • Deploy and operate the services the team develops

What you need

  • Undergraduate degree in Computer Science or a STEM field, or a strong alternative path
  • Experience with Linux (Debian or Ubuntu preferred)
  • Ability to provide insightful code reviews and set technical quality standards
  • An eye for accessibility and performance
  • Professional written and spoken English and strong communication skills
  • Able to travel twice a year for company events of up to two weeks each

Applicants must be based in: Nigeria.
