Finance Reporting Associate

Remote WorldwidePosted 9 days ago

A remote finance role supporting financial reporting, reconciliations and day-to-day finance operations for a data and analytics-focused organisation. The position has a strong emphasis on tax compliance, particularly the Nigerian tax system, where the holder would turn tax requirements into practical internal processes. It suits an accounting or finance graduate with a few years of experience who enjoys structured reporting, attention to detail and building sound controls in a fast-paced setting.

What you'll do

  • Manage and implement tax compliance processes, including filings, payments and supporting documentation
  • Prepare regular financial reports covering revenue, expenses, cash flow and key metrics
  • Reconcile bank accounts, payment platforms and internal financial records
  • Track revenue, outstanding balances, refunds and instalment plans
  • Support budgeting, forecasting and budget vs. actual analysis
  • Identify opportunities to improve finance processes, controls and reporting

What you need

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Business Administration or a related field
  • 2–3 years of experience in finance, accounting, tax or financial reporting
  • Strong practical knowledge of the Nigerian tax system
  • Experience implementing or managing tax compliance processes is highly preferred
  • Strong Excel or Google Sheets skills, including formulas, pivot tables and reporting
  • Professional qualification such as ICAN, ACCA or CITN is an advantage

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
