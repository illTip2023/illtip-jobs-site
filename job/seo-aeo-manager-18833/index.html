SEO/AEO Manager

Remote USFull-timePosted 4 days ago

A fully remote role managing organic search and AI answer-engine visibility across several client accounts. The work blends traditional SEO with optimisation for tools such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Gemini. The organisation is a US-focused digital marketing agency that values flexibility, diversity and professional growth. The role suits an experienced SEO professional who enjoys strategy, hands-on implementation, testing new approaches and building strong client relationships through clear reporting. Applicants must be based in the United States.

What you'll do

  • Develop and execute SEO and AEO strategies aligned with each client's business goals
  • Diagnose organic and AI search opportunities through audits, data analysis and performance tracking
  • Optimise content and site structure for search engines and large language models, including structured data and entity clarity
  • Design and run tests to improve organic performance and AI citations
  • Manage client relationships through regular meetings, detailed reporting and quarterly planning
  • Use in-house and third-party tools to support research and report results across traditional and AI search

What you need

  • 4+ years of experience in SEO strategy and implementation
  • Hands-on experience optimising for AI search and answer engines
  • Broad SEO skills across technical, content, keyword research, structured data and digital PR or link building
  • Experience in a client-facing digital marketing role
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Experience with AI visibility tracking tools is a plus

Applicants must be based in: United States.
