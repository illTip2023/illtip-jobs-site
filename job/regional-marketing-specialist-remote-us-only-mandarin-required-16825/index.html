Regional Marketing Specialist (Remote - US only, Mandarin Required)

Remote - US onlyPart-time, with a possible move to full-time after 3 to 6 months$25/hr–$35/hrPosted 3 days ago

Pay: $25/hr - $35/hr (hourly rate). Candidates must be based in the US. Periodic travel to San Jose, California for training and team activities. Full-time base pay of $80,000 - $100,000 per year upon conversion. This role is for an online K-12 education company that serves families across the United States, with a focus on math and English language arts programs for elementary students. The Regional Marketing Specialist will build a close understanding of local parent communities and education resources, then turn those insights into content, community initiatives, and local events. Over time, the role can grow into ownership of a geographic market.

What you'll do

  • Research local schools, education resources, parent communities, and market trends to identify real family needs and marketing opportunities.
  • Join and actively take part in local parent groups on Chinese-language messaging platforms, building genuine relationships and turning conversations into usable insights.
  • Create and test content for Chinese-language social platforms based on parent needs and local findings.
  • Plan and run local parent events, community partnerships, and education-related initiatives.
  • Own marketing projects end to end, from planning and coordination through execution, follow-up, and outcome review.

What you need

  • Experience in marketing, content, community management, education, social media, or a related field.
  • Fluent English and Mandarin Chinese, with the ability to build relationships with parents naturally in both languages.
  • Strong user sense, with the ability to understand audience needs and behaviors.
  • Strong research, learning, and problem-solving skills.
  • Proactive, detail-oriented, and execution-focused, with a strong sense of ownership.
  • Must be based in the United States and able to travel periodically to San Jose, California.

Applicants must be based in: United States.
