iOS Engineer (Creative App)

Remote WorldwidePosted 8 days ago

A remote, worldwide role building the iOS version of a creative audio app for a fast-moving AI audio company. The team works in lean, autonomous groups, uses AI across its work, and values impact over job titles. Suits an experienced native iOS engineer who enjoys shipping features, learning from experiments, and shaping how a product grows. Candidates comfortable with ambiguity and changing priorities will feel at home. The salary range is stated in the original listing and is not repeated here.

What you'll do

  • Ship features for the Creative App on iOS that integrate with AI-driven audio models
  • Decide where focused bets or incremental improvements will most move the product
  • Design and interpret experiments to guide what to build next
  • Work closely with design, product and research teams
  • Help shape the app's architecture and technical direction

What you need

  • Strong native iOS development with Swift and SwiftUI
  • A track record of shipping features that improved engagement
  • Experience designing and running A/B tests
  • Comfortable with ambiguity and fast-changing priorities
  • Experience owning performance, quality and responsiveness in a production app
  • Bonus: experience with audio playback or streaming on iOS

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
