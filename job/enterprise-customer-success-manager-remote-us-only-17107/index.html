Enterprise Customer Success Manager (Remote - US only)

Remote, United StatesFull-timePosted 5 days ago

This role sits within the customer success team of a fast-growing safety and risk software company that provides threat intelligence and emergency communication tools to businesses of all sizes. The Enterprise Customer Success Manager owns relationships with large, multi-national accounts and works as a strategic partner to help them reach their goals with the platform. The position works closely with implementation, support, marketing, and sales teams.

What you'll do

  • Build and manage relationships with a portfolio of enterprise customers, supporting adoption, retention, and expansion.
  • Run regular touchpoints and Quarterly Business Reviews that show customers their realized value and recommend new features.
  • Assess risk for each account and recommend best practices to protect retention.
  • Consult with customers on data automation options, including configurations for matrixed organizations.
  • Coordinate across internal teams as the single point of accountability for account execution, and keep executives informed with timely escalations.
  • Feed customer software requirements back to product management and train customers on new releases.

What you need

  • At least 3 years of experience as a Customer Success Manager, including 1-2+ years supporting enterprise customers.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to work with a wide range of customer profiles.
  • Proficiency with Salesforce and Excel; experience with Gainsight is a plus.
  • Strong project management, prioritization, analytical, and negotiation skills.
  • Experience in emergency communications or risk response is nice to have.
  • Must be authorized to work in the United States; the employer does not sponsor work visas.

Applicants must be based in: United States.
