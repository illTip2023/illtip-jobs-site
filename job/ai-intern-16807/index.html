AI Intern

Remote WorldwideInternshipPosted 4 days ago

This is a remote internship within a large corporate organisation's central AI team. You would work alongside business colleagues to turn practical ideas into working AI tools, helping to spot where AI could save time or support better decisions. The role involves designing, prototyping and piloting solutions with modern AI tools, as well as creating learning materials and demos that help colleagues adopt AI. It suits a student or recent graduate in a technical field who enjoys bridging technical and business thinking and communicating clearly. Some US states are excluded from remote eligibility.

What you'll do

  • Partner with business teams to identify and define AI use cases that improve efficiency or decision-making
  • Prototype, test and deploy solutions using low-code AI frameworks, large language models and orchestration frameworks
  • Support AI literacy materials, demos and internal showcases to encourage adoption

What you need

  • Pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in a technical field such as computer science, statistics or engineering
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to translate technical concepts into business value
  • Experience with AI/ML applications and/or data science
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills

Open to applicants anywhere in the world.
