Lead Product Manager, Image, Video & Formats (Remote - EMEA)

Remote - EMEAFull-timePosted 2 days ago

Candidates must be based in, and work from, their home country within the region. This is a senior product leadership role at an advertising technology company that uses agentic AI to produce ads for some of the largest advertisers in the world. The company's agents create finished advertising across video, stills, print, out-of-home, display, email, product pages and audio. You will own whether that output is ready for a client's creative team to sign off and place in market without changes. You will also lead a team of engineers and agent architects building the core capabilities behind these formats.

What you'll do

  • Own the charter for image, video and format capabilities, including the problem it solves, success metrics and a clear list of what is in and out of scope.
  • Define a ship-ready quality bar for each format, written in terms a creative director would recognise and specific enough to judge every agent-made ad against.
  • Turn that bar into scaled evaluations that score agent output, and keep those evaluations aligned with expert creative judgement.
  • Use evaluation results to prioritise work: improving agents where success rates lag and building missing formats or capabilities.
  • Own video craft (opening seconds, pacing, cutdowns, placement versions, localisation, voice-over, dubbing and lip-sync), along with still and product craft and structured layouts and typography. Set direction for audio, 3D and new formats.
  • Work with client teams and their agent architects so that shared capabilities are adapted to each client and improvements flow back into the core.

What you need

  • Around eight years of product management experience, with a strong grounding in creative production.
  • Evidence of defining a measurable quality bar for creative work and checking that the measures match expert opinion.
  • A track record of using quality metrics to direct a team's roadmap and of shipping creative output at volume across markets, formats and placements.
  • Experience influencing product managers, designers and engineers without direct line management over them.
  • Comfort running the full product lifecycle, from discovery and written hypotheses through to post-launch review.
  • Experience using generative AI tools in real production work is a plus.

Applicants must be based in: United Kingdom, Germany, Netherlands, Spain, Poland, Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, Egypt, Uae.
