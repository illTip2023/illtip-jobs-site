Admin Officer

Port Harcourt, Rivers State NigeriaFull-timePosted 8 days ago

An organised administrative role based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, supporting the daily running of a resources and oil and gas services business. The position suits a detail-minded administrator who enjoys keeping documents, schedules and records in good order, and who can support management and business development work. Exposure to the energy sector is a plus, and the role offers a steady, office-based environment where accuracy and discretion are valued.

What you'll do

  • Manage office administration, correspondence, filing and documentation
  • Assist with bid and tender documents, client files and vendor registrations
  • Maintain records of certifications, compliance documents, clients, staff and contracts
  • Coordinate management schedules, meetings and follow-ups
  • Handle office supplies procurement and track vendor and contractor payments
  • Provide administrative support across HR, finance and operations

What you need

  • HND or Bachelor's degree in Business Administration or a related field
  • 2–4 years of administrative experience
  • Oil and gas or engineering experience preferred
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel and Outlook
  • Strong organisational, communication and multitasking skills
  • High level of discretion and attention to detail
