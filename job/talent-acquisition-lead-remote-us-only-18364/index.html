Talent Acquisition Lead (Remote - US only)

Remote - US onlyFull-time$125,000–$175,000 per yearPosted 10 days ago

A venture-backed healthcare technology company is building technology-supported programs that train and support family caregivers to deliver high-quality care at home. The company works with health plans and public-sector partners and is in a fast-growing, Series C stage. This role leads recruiting for corporate G&A and clinical leadership roles, and reports to the Director of Talent Acquisition.

What you'll do

  • Lead, mentor and develop a team of recruiters, setting clear expectations for accountability and ownership.
  • Design and run scalable talent programs, such as employer branding for non-technical roles, structured interviewer training, diversity partnerships and internal referral programs.
  • Advise G&A and clinical executives on headcount priorities, organizational design, and closing strategies for senior hires.
  • Hold a targeted set of critical requisitions personally while overseeing the team's multi-channel sourcing for competitive corporate and healthcare talent.
  • Refine interview assessments, clinical evaluation frameworks and ATS practices to keep the hiring bar high and the candidate experience consistent.
  • Use recruiting metrics to tell the talent story, remove bottlenecks and inform executive decisions.

What you need

  • 8+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience, with a focus on G&A, operations and clinical leadership in fast-paced or high-growth settings.
  • At least 1 year of directly managing recruiters or individual contributors and owning or scaling talent programs; experience building a function from the ground up is a plus.
  • Proven track record sourcing and closing senior hires (Lead through VP level) across finance, people, legal, clinical operations and medical leadership.
  • Solid understanding of healthcare delivery models and corporate scaling.
  • Strong data skills for recruiting analytics and executive-level reporting.
  • Must be authorized to work in the United States; the company participates in E-Verify.

Applicants must be based in: United States.
