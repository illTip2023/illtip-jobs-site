What you do

Film one hands-on task from start to finish (1–20 minutes) and describe in English what your hands are doing, the whole time.

Most needed: repairs and DIY, tailoring, sewing and textiles, tech (phone or computer repairs). Crafts, hobbies, arts and home tasks also fit.

Checklist: every clip

  • Phone mounted on your head or chest (a strap, headband or cap clip works)
  • Both hands in view for the whole clip
  • Talk the whole time in clear English: say what you are doing and why
  • Start the task within 15 seconds of pressing record
  • Record in 1080p or higher, in good light
  • Do the full task, beginning to end

Clips that get rejected

  • Silence or long pauses in the narration
  • Hands leaving the shot, or the camera pointing away
  • Other people's faces, or documents and screens with personal details
  • Sports, driving, or general walk-through / overview videos
  • Waiting more than 15 seconds before starting

Only approved clips are paid. A short practice run before you record helps a lot.

Sample narration

"I'm going to replace a button on this shirt. First I'm cutting off the loose thread with my right hand… now I'm threading the needle, holding the needle in my left hand and the thread in my right…"
